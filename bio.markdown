I am a Manchester-based artist working across film, graphic design, writing, sound and discursive event organising. Her research interests centre primarily on digital anthropology; how emerging digital infrastructures impact human populations, and how individuals and societies in turn shape the technologies we use. I am currently 1/7 of the n-Space cohort at Somerset House Studios, a new space for interdisciplinary experimentation across art and technology, bringing together artists, technologists and academics. 

I am 1/2 of duo Y7 with my partner Declan Colquitt. Y7 specialise in the use and critique of emerging technologies to produce films, write, curate and design. Our interview-led documentary Undead Internet Theory was released in October 2025 after being exhibited at Patchlab, Krakow, and is currently available for viewing on DIS.art. We have shown work in London, New York, Dubai, Krakow, Amsterdam, Melbourne, Manchester, Tehran and Edinburgh.
