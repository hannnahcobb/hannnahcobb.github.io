I am a Manchester-based artist working across film, graphic design, writing, sound and discursive event organising. My research interests centre primarily on digital anthropology; how emerging digital infrastructures impact human populations, and how individuals and societies in turn shape the technologies we use. 

I am 1/7 of [n-Space](https://www.somersethouse.org.uk/somerset-house-studios/residencies-and-opportunities/n-space), a new space at Somerset House Studios for interdisciplinary experimentation across art and technology, bringing together artists, technologists and academics. I am 1/2 of [Y7](https://www.instagram.com/_y7_y7_y7_/?hl=en) with my partner Declan Colquitt. Y7 specialise in the use and critique of emerging technologies to produce films, write, curate and design. Our interview-led documentary [Undead Internet Theory](https://dis.art/) was released in October 2025 after being exhibited at Patchlab, Krakow, and is currently available for viewing on DIS.art.
